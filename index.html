The coach gives you advice for your own moves: the green arrow and the red alternatives.
Coach strength 20 is full strength. Lower it if you want advice closer to a human level. Engine memory is shared with the bot.
The bot is your opponent in “Play against the bot”, and both players in “Bot vs bot”.
“Longest move” is only an upper limit. For each move the bot picks a time between fastest and longest, usually on the quick side, and with the box ticked it plays as soon as its choice is stable. A 10 second limit therefore does not mean 10 seconds per move. In Bot vs bot the first Elo plays White and “Black bot Elo” plays Black.
With auto-fit on, the board is sized to your screen. Turn it off to use the fixed pixel size. On narrow screens the panel always moves below the board.
Click “Share screen” and pick the window or tab with the chess game. Everything else is automatic.
Sets every setting to its recommended value: Coach mode, full-strength advice with 2 s of thinking, a balanced 2000 Elo bot, all three arrows, fast screen refresh and the standard look. Your side, start position and clock stay as they are.
To move a preset to a new tab or another device: copy the code, open the app there and paste it below. It is applied automatically.
